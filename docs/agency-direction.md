# Agency design direction — 7 November 2026

## Research

- Paper Tiger: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/paper-tiger and https://www.papertiger.com/ — Awwwards Site of the Day, 17 January 2026. Live browser review showed oversized condensed black display lettering, very small utility navigation and large image-led work. Its inspected Latest Work heading uses weight 900 with a compact line height. The relevant principle is a strong display/utility contrast, not a copy of its font or layout.
- Studio Size: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/studio-size and https://studio-size.com/ — Awwwards Honorable Mention, 16 April 2020. Reviewed the current live studio site: simple typography, small navigation, expansive spacing and large, softly rounded project media. Its current design may differ from the awarded 2020 version. A rotating service phrase appears in its main heading.
- Unseen Studio: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/unseen-studio and https://unseen.co/ — Awwwards Site of the Day, 6 February 2023. The Awwwards entry highlights gallery transitions and menu effects. The current WebGL experience remained on its loader in this browser, so it was not used as verified evidence for mobile layout or performance.

## Applied interpretation

Use Bricolage Grotesque at weight 800 for display type, with its condensed width axis for the uppercase hero. Pair it with small service/location metadata and utility navigation. Keep the existing beige/red brand and four corners. Add consistent 24px mobile / 32px desktop media radii, larger work images, generous captions and a circular project action. A restrained outlined service capsule and service chips add hierarchy without boxing every section. Use a solid rounded email action for contact.

Preserve exact frame navigation, slow letter reveals, splash/menu behavior, reduced-motion support, editable copy, image crops and publishing. No database migration or portfolio facts are needed for this visual change. Existing project covers remain illustrative placeholders.
